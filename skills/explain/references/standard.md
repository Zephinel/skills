# Standard sources and verification limits

ASD-STE100 is a controlled-language standard for English technical documentation. It combines writing rules with a controlled dictionary. This skill provides an explanation workflow. It does not reproduce the full standard or dictionary and is not a compliance checker.

## Official sources

- [STEMG website](https://www.asd-ste100.org/): verify the edition and maintaining organization.
- [About STE](https://www.asd-ste100.org/about_STE.html): understand the standard's purpose and structure.
- [Official download entry](https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_downloads.html): obtain the applicable edition.

On 2026-10-06, retrieved official search snippets listed Issue 9, dated 2025-01-15. Requests for the full pages and standard PDF returned HTTP 403. This skill therefore does not present individual rules or approved vocabulary as fully verified against Issue 9. When exact editions or rules matter, retrieve the official material again or use a lawfully provided user copy.

## Strict English verification

Identify the requested edition and obtain its complete rules and dictionary. Distinguish procedural from descriptive text, then check the applicable sentence, length, structure, and vocabulary requirements. Check each word's meaning and part of speech, and the basis for using technical names or technical verbs; matching word forms alone is insufficient.

Report the scope checked, issues found, and missing evidence. With only summaries, recollection, or a third-party checker, retain the status “not fully verified.” A Chinese adaptation is not evidence of compliance with the English standard.
